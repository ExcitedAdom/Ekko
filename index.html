Ekko

Who is Ekko?

Ekko, my fav character in Arcane series, is also called "The Boy Savior". He is a young scientist from Zaun, strong fighter towards justice, and a very good duo for Jinx.

Ekko's powers

Ekko has the power to manipulate time; he invented a device called the Z-Drive, which allows him to go back in time for 4 seconds. This ability may show not that much, but it nearly saved the Arcane universe (won't spoil it for you).
